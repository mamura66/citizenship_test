# Official photo sources — public domain, verified

All four images are official U.S. government works (17 U.S.C. §105), confirmed public
domain on their Wikimedia Commons file pages before download. Re-verify alongside
`national-dynamic.json` whenever an officeholder changes — a new officeholder needs a
new photo from the same kind of primary source, not a placeholder.

| File | Subject | Source page |
|---|---|---|
| `president.jpg` | Donald Trump, official presidential portrait | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Official_Presidential_Portrait_of_President_Donald_J._Trump_(2025).jpg |
| `vp.jpg` | JD Vance, official VP portrait | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:January_2025_Official_Vice_Presidential_Portrait_of_JD_Vance.jpg |
| `speaker.jpg` | Mike Johnson, official Speaker portrait | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Speaker_Mike_Johnson_Official_Portrait.jpg |
| `chiefjustice.jpg` | John Roberts, official SCOTUS portrait | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Official_roberts_CJ.jpg |

Governors' official state-provided photos are intentionally NOT included here — states
are not bound by 17 U.S.C. §105 the way the federal government is, so each state's
photo-usage rules need individual confirmation before use (see docs/LEGAL_REVIEW.md §7).
