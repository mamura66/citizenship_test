I paid but the app still shows the paywall

Open Settings → Restore purchases. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID, so it recovers on any device signed in to that account. You are never charged twice: the unlock is a one-time non-consumable purchase, so Apple blocks a second charge and restores the original instead.

I reinstalled the app and lost my progress

Your scores, starred questions, name and interview date are stored only on your device, which is what keeps them private — so deleting the app removes them. Your purchase is not lost: tap Restore purchases after reinstalling.

Which test version should I use?

It depends on when you filed Form N-400. Use the 2025 test if you filed on or after the date USCIS specifies for it; otherwise use the 2008 test. Switch in Settings → Civics test. Check your own USCIS notice if you are unsure — the app cannot determine this for you.

An officeholder's name looks out of date

Governors and national officeholders change with elections. We verify them against the National Governors Association and the official whitehouse.gov, supremecourt.gov and house.gov pages before each release. If you spot one that is wrong, email us and we will correct it.

Why isn't my senator or representative listed?

Those answers depend on your exact congressional district, and we would rather show nothing than a name that might be wrong for you. Look yours up at senate.gov and house.gov.

Does the app record my voice?

No. The mock interview only speaks, using your device's built-in text-to-speech. The microphone is never accessed.
