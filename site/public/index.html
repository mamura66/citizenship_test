What it does

Built around the way the test is actually organized.

Every citizenship test is published as a set of official sections. So is everything here — you work through one subject at a time rather than shuffling a pile of questions, and each section tells you what it covers before you start.

Study by section

Flashcards reveal every officially accepted answer. Where a question asks for one but the authority accepts many, you see all of them and the card says any one will do.

Practice in the real format

Each test matches the question count and pass mark of your country's official test. Answer and the option turns green or red immediately — no hunting for a verdict.

Rehearse it out loud

The interview is spoken by a person. Questions are read aloud so the first time you hear them isn't across a desk from an officer. Your microphone is never used.

Questions

Straight answers.

United States: which test version applies to me?

The version that applies depends on your Form N-400 filing date and the rules in effect for your application. Your USCIS appointment notice states which test you'll take. The app carries both in full, and you switch between them in Settings — we deliberately don't guess on your behalf, because getting it wrong would mean studying the wrong material.

Is this an official government app?

No. It's an independent study aid, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any government or immigration authority. The questions come from each country's own published test materials — for the United States, works of the U.S. Government published by USCIS, which are in the public domain.

Does it give legal or immigration advice?

No. It teaches the civics test. It doesn't advise on your case, prepare or submit any form, or take part in your application. For advice about your situation, speak to a licensed immigration attorney or an accredited representative.

Does the mock interview record my voice?

No. It only speaks, using your phone's built-in text-to-speech. The microphone is never accessed, and no audio is recorded, stored or transmitted.

I paid and then got a new phone. Do I pay again?

No. The unlock is a one-time non-consumable purchase tied to your Apple ID. Tap Restore Purchases in Settings on the new device. Your study progress, though, lives only on the old phone — that's the trade-off for keeping it off our servers.

Are answers about current officeholders kept up to date?

Yes. A few questions ask who currently holds an office, and those change with elections. Every one is checked against official government sources before each release — for the United States, the National Governors Association plus whitehouse.gov, supremecourt.gov and house.gov. If you ever spot one that looks wrong, tell us through the support page and we'll correct it.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in your browser, on a phone or a computer. An iPhone app is on the way, and Android after that.
