2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE

US Citizenship Test 2026-Pulse is a study aid for lawful permanent residents (green card
holders) preparing for the naturalization civics test, which is taken during the USCIS
Form N-400 interview.

The problem it solves: an applicant must be ready to answer questions drawn from the
official civics pool - up to 128 questions on the 2025 test, 100 on the 2008 test - plus
four questions whose answers depend on the state they live in. The official study
materials are PDFs and printed lists, which are difficult to study from and give the
applicant no sense of whether they are ready.

The value it provides: the official questions in a study format organised by the same
sections USCIS uses; practice tests in the real interview format and pass threshold;
a spoken-question mode so the applicant hears the questions aloud before interview day;
and a readiness measure calculated only from their own completed practice tests.

3. SETTING UP AND ACCESSING THE MAIN FEATURES

No account, no registration, no login and no credentials are required. There is nothing
to sign in to and no demo account is needed. Every feature is reachable immediately
after install.

On first launch there is a three-screen introduction: a welcome screen, an optional
first name and interview date, and a screen describing how the app handles data. Both
personal fields can be skipped.

The app then has five tabs:

- Home - readiness gauge, recent practice scores, and a countdown to the interview date
  if one was entered.
- Study - flashcards for every official question. Tap "All topics" to study one official
  USCIS section at a time. "Read question" uses the device's own text-to-speech.
- Practice - a practice test in the real interview format. Choose the number of questions
  and the pass threshold, then answer.
- Interview - spoken practice. The app reads a question aloud, the user answers out loud
  to themselves and then self-grades.
- Settings - appearance, civics test version (2025 or 2008), the user's state, Restore
  Purchases, and the privacy and disclaimer text.

No sample files are required. All content ships inside the app.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS

- Apple StoreKit / the App Store - processes the in-app purchase. We never see payment
  details.
- RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) - in-app purchase management. It receives an anonymous
  identifier generated per installation and the purchase record for this app. It does
  not receive any name, email address, or anything the user typed into the app. We use
  anonymous app user IDs only and never call its login API.
- Apple's on-device speech synthesis (AVSpeechSynthesizer) - reads questions aloud. It
  runs entirely on the device and makes no network request. The microphone is never
  accessed, and no audio is recorded, stored or transmitted.
- Expo / EAS - build tooling only. Not present at runtime.

There is no backend server, no authentication service, no analytics or crash-reporting
SDK, no advertising SDK, and no AI or machine-learning service. All civics content is
bundled in the binary, so the app makes no network requests to fetch content. The only
network traffic is the purchase check to RevenueCat and Apple.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES

There are none. The app behaves identically in every region and is available worldwide
in English only. The subject matter is United States civics, but nothing is gated,
altered or hidden by region. The in-app purchase is the same single product at the same
price tier in every territory.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL

The app is a study aid. It does not provide legal advice, immigration advice, or any
immigration service. It does not prepare or submit any government form and takes no part
in any application. This is stated inside the app on the onboarding screen and in
Settings > About, and in the App Store description.

All civics questions, their official answers, and the reading and writing vocabulary
lists are works of the United States Government published by U.S. Citizenship and
Immigration Services. They are in the public domain under 17 U.S.C. section 105 and are
taken from USCIS's published test materials at uscis.gov - the 2025 civics test
(Form M-1778) and the 2008 100-question civics test. No permission is required to
reproduce works of the U.S. Government.

The four photographs of national officeholders are likewise public-domain works of the
U.S. Government, with per-image sources recorded in our project files.

The app is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by USCIS,
the Department of Homeland Security, or any government agency. It does not use any
government seal, logo or trademark, and does not use "USCIS" or any agency name in its
app name or icon. The non-affiliation statement appears in three places in the product:
the onboarding screen, Settings > About, and the App Store description.

7. WHAT USERS CAN BUY, AND HOW TO REACH THE PURCHASE

There is one in-app purchase: "Lifetime Access" (product ID lifetime_access), a
non-consumable priced at $9.99. It is a single one-time purchase. There is no
subscription and no recurring charge.

Free forever, with no purchase required: all flashcards for every official question,
study by topic, read-aloud, the user's state-specific answers, the readiness screen,
and one complete practice test.

The purchase unlocks: unlimited practice tests, and the full mock interview mode.

To reach the purchase screen:

1. Open the Practice tab and complete one full practice test through to the results
   screen. This first test is free.
2. Start a second practice test. The purchase screen appears at that point.

There are two other routes to the same screen: Settings > "View lifetime unlock", or the
Interview tab > "Unlock Mock Interview".

Restore Purchases is available both on the purchase screen and in Settings, since the
purchase is a non-consumable tied to the customer's Apple ID.

Please note: on a fresh install, the purchase screen appears after the first free
practice test, as described above. On our own test device the purchase had already been
made, so the app may appear already unlocked in the recording; the purchase screen is
shown there via Settings > "View lifetime unlock", which is the same screen.
