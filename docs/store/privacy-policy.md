# Privacy Policy — US Citizenship Test 2026-Pulse

**Last updated: 5 September 2026**

## The short version

This app does not ask who you are and does not have accounts. Everything you do in it —
your name, your interview date, your state, your practice scores, your starred questions —
is stored on your own device and is never sent to us. We could not look up your data if we
wanted to, because we never receive it.

## What stays on your device

The following is saved locally on your phone and nowhere else:

- the first name you optionally enter
- your naturalization interview date, if you choose to add one
- your selected state or territory
- your practice test scores and history
- the questions you star for review
- your appearance and civics-test-version preferences

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no backup on our side and no way to restore
it other than your own device backups.

## What leaves your device

**Purchases.** The one-time lifetime purchase is processed by **Apple**. We never see
your payment details. To know whether you have purchased, we use **RevenueCat**, a
purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier generated for
your installation and your purchase record for this app. It does not receive your name,
email address, or anything you typed into the app. See
[RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) and
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

That is the only data that leaves your device.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect analytics or usage tracking.
- We do not use advertising or advertising identifiers.
- We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not ask for permission to,
  because we have nothing to track you with.
- We do not sell or share personal information. We have none to sell.
- We do not record, transmit, or store audio. The mock interview uses your device's own
  text-to-speech to read questions aloud; your microphone is never accessed. If speech
  scoring is ever added, it will ask for separate, explicit consent first.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone,
including children.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct,
or delete on request. To erase everything the app has stored, delete the app. Your purchase
remains recoverable through Apple with "Restore Purchases".

## Not legal advice, and not affiliated with the government

This app is an independent study tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored
by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), the Department of Homeland Security,
or any government agency. The civics questions and vocabulary lists are U.S. government
works published by USCIS and are in the public domain. The app provides general educational
information about the naturalization test and does not provide legal advice.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
